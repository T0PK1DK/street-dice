How this curb runs

  • Pass-and-play: tap a player to hold their stack.
  • Shooter posts the line. Everybody else can fade a piece until it's covered.
  • Come-out: 7 or 11 the pass wins even money. 2, 3, or 12 the fade scoops it. Anything else is the point.
  • Then the shooter has to hit that point before a 7. Seven-out and the dice walk.
  • Craps on the come-out loses the line, but the shooter keeps the rocks.
  • Room bets are player-to-player. One soul offers, another covers at true odds.
  • Point vs 7: 4/10 pays 2:1, 5/9 pays 3:2, 6/8 pays 6:5.
  • Next-roll 7 is 5:1, 11 is 17:1, any craps is 8:1. Hard 4/10 is 8:1, hard 6/8 is 10:1.
