How this curb runs

  • Pass the phone. The handoff screen tells you who is up.
  • Stack chips ($1 / $5 / $10 / $20), undo the last chip or last bet, or clear the handful, then confirm. Shooter posts the line; everybody else fades it.
  • Come-out: 7 or 11 pass wins even money. 2, 3, or 12 the fade scoops it. Anything else is the point.
  • Then hit that point before a 7. Seven-out and the dice walk. Craps lose the line, shooter keeps the rocks.
  • Room tickets (he hits / he don't) and side tickets (next 7/11, any craps, hardways): one player offers, another covers at true odds — 4/10 2:1, 5/9 3:2, 6/8 6:5, next 7 5:1, 11 17:1, craps 8:1, hard 4/10 8:1, hard 6/8 10:1.
  • Tap or hold the throw bar, or flick the alley. The number is decided first; the dice just land on it.
